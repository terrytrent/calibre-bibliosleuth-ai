# BiblioSleuth AI for calibre

BiblioSleuth AI researches exact editions with OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, or LM
Studio plus provider-hosted search or a user-managed SearXNG service, then lets
you review and apply evidence-backed metadata in calibre.

It can propose title, authors, series and series index, tags, identifiers, publication date, publisher, and a formatted library-quality description. Nothing is written until you approve it, and the EPUB file itself is never modified.
